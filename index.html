Outpatient Care with Continuity

Keeping patient care connected across visits

MediLens is an early-stage health technology startup working to connect every healthcare provider to India’s Digital Health Infrastructure (ABDM), so patient care can stay continuous across doctor visits.

We call this Connected Care.

Outpatient care is often episodic

Doctors work with incomplete patient history

Patient history is split across paper files, phone messages, and PDFs - leaving doctors to connect it all.

Patients have questions after they leave the clinic

Patients often call with repeat questions during busy OPD hours.

Clinic tools aren’t designed for good follow-up care

Handwritten notes, scattered EMR entries, and post-visit calls make managing follow-ups difficult.

Good outpatient care depends on continuity — knowing what happened before, what’s happening now, and what comes next.

What we call Connected Care

Connected Care is an approach to outpatient care that keeps patient context up to date across clinics, supports patients between visits, and helps doctors build familiarity with their patients to deliver more effective care.

What Connected Care looks like in practice

Complete Patient Context

Consultations start with full context - who the patient is, what was done before, and how they responded.

Guidance Between Visits

Post-visit calls from patients are organised and manageable, without burdening clinics.

Continuity of Care

Familiarity with a patient’s case and treatment response over time helps doctors deliver more effective care.

Intelligent Clinic Support

Clinic systems are designed for the pace and realities of Indian outpatient clinics.

ABHA & Insurance Ready

Ready for India's digital health IDs (ABHA) and growing OPD insurance coverage.
