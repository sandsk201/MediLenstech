Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 25, 2025

Introduction

MediLens Technologies Private Limited (“MediLens”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) respects the importance of privacy and responsible handling of information, especially in the context of healthcare.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you visit our website or contact us.

1. Information We Collect

At this stage, MediLens is primarily an informational website.

We may collect:

  • Basic contact details (such as name and email) that you voluntarily submit through the Contact Us form.
  • Limited, non-identifiable website usage data (such as page visits) to understand interest and improve the site

2. What We Do Not Collect

We do not:

  • Collect or process medical records or health data through this website
  • Provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment
  • Sell, rent, or share personal information for marketing purposes

3. How Information Is Used

Any information you choose to share with us is used only to:

  • Respond to inquiries about careers, partnerships, or early collaboration
  • Communicate about MediLens where relevant to your inquiry
  • Improve the clarity and usefulness of our website

4. Data Responsibility

We aim to handle all information responsibly and with appropriate safeguards. As MediLens evolves, our privacy practices will be updated to reflect applicable Indian laws and digital health standards.

5. Updates to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time as our website and offerings evolve. The latest version will always be available on this page.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out using the Contact Us page on our website.
