Connected Care for Outpatient Clinics

From one-time consultations to continuous care

How Connected Care helps

BEFORE THE VISIT

Complete Patient Context

Patient’s past prescriptions, tests, and treatments from across clinics are ready for Doctor's review.

DURING THE VISIT

Intelligent Clinic Support

Important case history is easy to refer to, and new notes are easy to add to the patient's file.

AFTER THE VISIT

Structured Patient Enquires

Post-visit patient enquiries and clarifications are organized and recognized as clinic work.

BEYOND THE VISIT

Continuity of Care

Doctor–patient relationships extend beyond individual visits, helping doctors know the patient’s case well.

What makes Connected Care

Clinic Intelligence System

Well-organised patient history and visit summaries that give each consultation a clear starting point.

Patient Companion App

Access to health reports, explanations in simple language, and a structured channel for follow-up clarifications.

Digital Records (ABDM–ABHA)

Aligned with India's digital health standards (ABDM), enabling doctors to view patient history from other clinics.

Claims Readiness

Simplified OPD claims management, helping clinics prepare for insured OPD care.

Incentive System

Recognition for all care provided - including in-clinic consultations and patient clarifications post visit.

Quiet AI

Systems that work alongside doctors, making digitization simpler and less burdensome.

Why clinics gain from Connected Care

Consultations become easier and faster

Doctors begin each consultation with clear patient history, reducing the chance of missing critical clinical context.

Fewer repeat calls during busy OPD hours

Post-visit patient questions are addressed through a structured channel, reducing interruptions during OPD hours.

Patients return regularly for follow-ups

Patients receive clear follow-up advice and timelines, encouraging timely return visits.

A steady OPD with regular patients

Continuity of care encourages patients to stay with the same clinic over time.

Modernization without fear

As India's healthcare becomes more digital, clinics need systems that simplify OPD work, not disrupt it.

Over 80 crore Indians now have digital health records (ABDM-ABHA)

As OPD insurance expands and public schemes like PM-JAY grow, clear documentation becomes essential

Over 1.6 lakh healthcare facilities use ABDM-compliant systems to share information securely

With the right systems and support, OPD clinics can provide better patient care and continuity.
